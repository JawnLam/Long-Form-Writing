## What it must NOT do

- Be stated by any character in a way that reads as the novel's thesis
- Resolve neatly
- Become a motif's stated meaning (the motif must remain physical; the theme must remain implied)
- Be the answer to the dramatic question (the dramatic question's answer is plot; the theme is *what is illuminated* by that answer)
