# <Manuscript Title> — Story Circle Overlay

> **Dan Harmon's eight-beat distillation of Joseph Campbell. A reading lens, NOT a writing prescription. Map your existing `_spine.md` onto these eight beats and see what shape your spine actually has. Divergence from the circle is information, not error.**

## The eight beats

The Story Circle goes around: a character is in a zone of comfort, wants something, enters an unfamiliar situation, adapts to it, gets what they wanted, pays a price for it, returns to the familiar situation, and is changed.

| # | Beat | Question | Maps to scene(s) |
|---|------|----------|------------------|
| 1 | **You** — character in their zone of comfort | Who are they, before? | |
| 2 | **Need** — they want something | What activates them? | |
| 3 | **Go** — they cross a threshold | What's the unfamiliar situation? | |
| 4 | **Search** — they adapt | What do they try / face? | |
| 5 | **Find** — they get what they wanted | What do they obtain? | |
| 6 | **Take** — they pay the price | What's the cost? | |
| 7 | **Return** — they come back to where they started | What's the homecoming? | |
| 8 | **Change** — they have changed | What's different about them now? | |

## Divergence notes

*Where this manuscript deliberately doesn't fit the circle, and why. Literary fiction often refuses the Change beat (beat 8) — the character is *less* changed than they expected, or changed in ways that aren't decoded. Document the divergence so it's a choice, not an accident.*

## How to use this overlay

- During OUTLINE: map the planned spine onto the circle; identify shape-gaps
- During READ-THROUGH: check whether each beat's question is being answered in the drafted prose, or whether the beat is structural-not-yet-prose
- During REVISE: when a chapter feels off, check which beat it should be carrying and what's missing

## Risks

- **Treating the circle as a checklist.** Save the Cat does this more egregiously; Story Circle is more forgiving but the risk is real. The circle is a lens for *seeing* shape, not a recipe for *producing* it.
- **Forcing your spine to fit when the spine is right and the circle is wrong.** Walk away from the overlay if it doesn't serve.
