## Purpose

Third worked example. Religious institution operating since 529 AD with explicit textual codification of practice (Rule of St. Benedict). Use to show how *explicit codification* of practice contributes to persistence — but also how the order's adaptive flexibility within the Rule is what makes it work over fifteen centuries.
