# Chapter 2 — Sarah Knows

## Purpose in the manuscript

POV switches to Sarah. The reader learns Sarah already knows about the debts (per [[_continuity]] information-state ledger — Sarah read her copy of Frank Mendel's letter in January). Sarah arrives at the vineyard, has the first dinner with Maya (during which the empty chair at the head of the table gets its first weight-bearing appearance, per the SCENE-AUDIT proposal in [[01-01-The-Approach]]). The frost-warning becomes an official forecast via Hector's pre-dawn call.

This chapter establishes Sarah's POV register — slightly longer cadenced sentences than Maya's, visual / kinetic interiority (she re-sees moments), the "okay" verbal tic that means *I have heard you and have not yet agreed.*

## Scenes in this chapter (planned)

1. Sarah's drive from the airport — Sarah's interior debate about whether to tell Maya tonight (Scene 2-1)
2. The first dinner — the empty chair; the conversation neither sister knows how to have (Scene 2-2)
3. Hector's pre-dawn forecast call — the frost-watch begins (Scene 2-3)

## Chapter-level value-arc

- **Opening state:** Sarah is resolved (during the drive) to tell Maya about the debts tonight, before either of them sleeps
- **Closing state:** Sarah has not told Maya. The frost-warning has arrived. The window for tonight's telling has closed; the window for telling at all has narrowed
