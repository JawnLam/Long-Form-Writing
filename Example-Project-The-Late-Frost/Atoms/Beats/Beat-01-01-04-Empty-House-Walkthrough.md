# Beat 01-01-04 — Empty House Walkthrough

## Purpose

Maya enters the empty house and walks through it. The house has been unoccupied since Daniel's death nine months ago. The atmospheric load: the house is paused, not abandoned. Coffee in the canister. Mail stack on the entry table (mostly junk; nothing from Frank Mendel — that letter is on the kitchen counter, beat 5). The chair at the head of the dining table is empty in a different way than it was when Maya last sat there for Thanksgiving four years ago.

This is the beat that does most of the chapter's atmospheric work. It is also the beat where the value-shift `from → to` (ignorant → suspecting) currently lands in the prose, which is the **SCENE-AUDIT flag**.

## Beat content (brief)

Maya enters. The pause. The kitchen. The dining room. The chair. The hallway to the bedrooms — she does not go down the hallway. She returns to the kitchen.

## Connections

- **Motif touch:** [[The-Empty-Chair]] — currently latent, but per SCENE-AUDIT flag this beat is the candidate for its first weight-bearing appearance (one chapter earlier than [[_outline]] currently has it)
- **Promises:** None directly planted in this beat (Promise 2 plants in Beat 5)

## SCENE-AUDIT flag — Session 003

**Issue:** This beat closes with the sentence "she knew something was off." That's an *asserted* value-shift, not a *dramatized* one. The reader is told Maya's suspicion arrived but not shown the sensory or perceptual moment of its arrival.

**Proposed revision direction (for next session):** Let the moment of suspicion land on a specific perceptual fulcrum. The candidate fulcrum: **the chair at the head of the dining table**. The chair is empty in a way that's *different from how it should be empty.* Specifically: it is not pushed in. Daniel always pushed it in. The chair is pulled out an inch and at a slight angle, as if someone has been sitting in it recently and left a meal partway. Sarah has not arrived. Hector would not sit at the head of the table. The chair is a small concrete impossibility. The suspicion-state lands there, on a thing rather than on a thought.

**This also gives [[The-Empty-Chair]] its first appearance with weight, which earns the motif faster than the current outline.**
