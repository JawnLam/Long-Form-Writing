# The Late Frost — Continuity Ledger

## World-rules

*The mechanics of the fictional world. For contemporary realism most defaults hold; this section records what is non-default or load-bearing.*

### Vineyard rules

- **Location:** A 28-acre estate vineyard in the Sonoma Valley AVA, near Glen Ellen. Family-owned since 1968 (purchased by the Hollis sisters' grandfather).
- **Plantings:** Cabernet Sauvignon (60%), Zinfandel (25%), Sauvignon Blanc (15%). The Cab is the financial engine; the Zin is the heritage block; the Sauv Blanc was planted by the father in the late 1990s.
- **Production:** ~3,500 cases per year. Boutique scale. Estate-only fruit.
- **Operations:** A vineyard manager (Hector Reyes, present continuously since 2009) lives on-site. Three part-time workers during pruning/harvest. The sisters do not work the vineyard daily; the manager runs operations and reports to the owners.
- **Frost defenses:** Wind machines on the lower blocks; a propane heating system installed in 2019 (still under loan); smudge pots in the Zin block (heritage equipment, marginally effective). The frost defenses are operated by the manager + the sisters during the frost-watch window.

### Late-frost mechanics (real-world, must be tracked accurately)

- **Frost window:** Late frost in Sonoma typically threatens between mid-March and late April, after bud-break. The novel takes place in early April.
- **Damage timeline:** A frost event below 28°F for >3 hours during bud-break destroys the season's primary fruiting buds. Secondary buds may produce a partial crop but typically at 30–50% of primary yield, with lower quality.
- **Damage assessment timeline:** Visible damage (browned shoots, blackened buds) appears within 24–48 hours of a frost event. Full assessment of the season's loss is not knowable until ~10 days later, when whether secondary buds will push becomes clear.
- **The novel's frost arc:** Three weeks. Week 1 = the threat and the first hard night. Week 2 = the assessment ambiguity. Week 3 = the resolution (the harvest is either lost, partially saved, or saved).

### Hollis family rules

- **The father (Daniel Hollis):** Died nine months before the novel opens. Long widowhood (the mother died when Maya was 14 and Sarah was 11). Daniel ran the vineyard alone from then until his death. He was a quiet, withholding man who showed affection through work, not words.
- **The estate:** Left jointly to Maya and Sarah, 50/50. The lawyer who managed the estate is the Hollis family's attorney of 30 years (Frank Mendel).
- **The debts:** Daniel borrowed against the vineyard three times in the last five years to keep operations afloat. Current outstanding: ~$1.2M against an estate valued at ~$3.4M. The vineyard is not in default but cannot continue operating at current loss-rate beyond one more season without either a capital infusion or a sale.
- **Frank Mendel's letter:** Sent ~3 months before the novel opens to both sisters. Sarah received hers and read it (it was forwarded to her current address). Maya's copy was sent to the vineyard address and is the unopened letter on the kitchen counter.
