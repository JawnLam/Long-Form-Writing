# The Late Frost — Style Sheet

> **The cartridge's style choices. State once; honor across 75,000 words. Consulted at BETA-PREP and at line-edit REVISE. F36 (dialogue-formatting drift) and F48 (style-sheet drift broadly) are what this file defends against.**

## Voice and register reminders

Close-third POV alternating Maya / Sarah by chapter. Present tense throughout. Plain sentences for the sister-dialogue; more layered for narrative interiority. Resist Hadley-influenced narrative distance; closer than that.

Per [[Maya-Hollis]] and [[Sarah-Hollis]]'s `lfw_pov_voice_register` fields, each POV's signature_moves and avoid_moves discipline carries this. The style sheet does not duplicate those — see the Character Items.

## Spelling

- **US spellings throughout.** "Grey" is acceptable for color only ("grey at the temples"); "gray" is the default for any other use
- **OK vs okay:** *okay* (the spelled form). Sarah's verbal tic is rendered as *okay*
- **Toward vs towards:** *toward*
- **Brand-name capitalization:**
  - *iPhone* (lowercase i)
  - *FaceTime* (one word, both capped)
- **Specific proper-noun spellings:**
  - *Cabernet Sauvignon* (both capped; *Cab* short form is acceptable in dialogue)
  - *Zinfandel* (cap Z); *Zin* (cap Z) for the short form
  - *Sauvignon Blanc* (both capped)
  - *Sonoma Valley* (both capped)
  - *Glen Ellen* (one *n* in Glen, two in Ellen)
  - *Highway 12* (capped, numeral)
  - *Mayacamas* / *Sonoma Mountains* (mountain ranges)

## Capitalization

- **Deity:** lowercase (none of the characters are religious enough to capitalize)
- **Titles before names:** capitalized (*Senator Hendrickson*) — though this novel has no titled characters in the prose
- **Vineyard names:** capitalized as proper nouns; *the Hollis vineyard* is lowercase when used as common reference, capitalized when it's a name (*Hollis Vineyards* — the legal entity)
- **The frost:** lowercase. *the late frost* is the novel's title in italics, but *the late frost* as event-reference in prose is lowercase

## Italics

- **Foreign words (Spanish):** italics on first appearance; not italicized thereafter. Sarah and Hector occasionally speak Spanish to each other; per [[Hector-Reyes]] convention, Spanish is rendered with the English translation provided in context, not as a glossed parenthetical
- **Emphasis:** rare; reserve for moments of genuine stress. Sarah's "okay" is *not* italicized (the pause and re-occurrence carry the emphasis)
- **Inner dialogue:** no italics. Maya and Sarah's interiority is rendered in close-third without italic distinction; the close-third frame makes the marker unnecessary
- **Titles of books / films / etc:** italics
- **Sound effects (rare):** italics

## Punctuation

- **Oxford comma:** yes
- **Em-dash:** unspaced (—like this—), used for interruption in dialogue, parenthetical pauses, and emphasized shifts
- **Ellipsis:** three dots (...) — never the ellipsis character (…). Used for trail-off; Sarah's "and..." trail-off is *and* followed by ellipsis only when the trail-off is visible (sometimes the trail-off is implicit and the dialogue just ends mid-phrase)
- **Quotation marks:** double for dialogue; single for quoted-within-quoted
- **Comma splice:** allowed sparingly, for prose-rhythm reasons (e.g., a Sarah-POV sentence might use a comma splice deliberately for the editing-rhythm effect)

## Numbers

- **Spelled-out vs numerals:** spelled out under 100 (*ninety-eight*, *forty-one*), numerals above (*1,200*, *3,500 cases*). Ages spelled out (*forty-one*). Years numeric (*2026*).
- **Time:** spelled out — *six in the morning*, *just past midnight*; if numeric form is needed, US convention (*6:00 am*, *11:30 pm*)
- **Dates:** *April 4*, not *April 4th* and not *4 April*

## Dialogue formatting

- **Tags:** mixed — *said* by default; action-beats often replace tags ("Maya put down the cup. 'I see.'"); other-than-*said* tags only when the manner of speech is doing actual work
- **Adverbs in tags:** disallowed (no *she said quietly*; replace with *she said* + action-beat or with a shorter sentence that carries the quietness)
- **Em-dash for interruption** / **Ellipsis for trail-off** — strict convention; do not interchange
- **Foreign-language quoted material (Spanish):** italics first instance only, with English context-provision
- **Inner dialogue:** no marker (per Italics section above)
- **Dialect:** no dialect rendering. Hector's English is grammatical; his Spanish is grammatical. No phonetic dialect spellings
