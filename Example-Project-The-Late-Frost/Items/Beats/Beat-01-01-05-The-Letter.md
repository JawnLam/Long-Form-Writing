# Beat 01-01-05 — The Letter

## Purpose

**Plant Promise 2 (the unopened letter from the lawyer).** Maya returns to the kitchen and sees, on the counter near the canister of coffee she just registered three minutes ago, an envelope she had not noticed on her first walkthrough. The envelope is from Frank Mendel's office. The return-address line is what Maya sees first. The envelope is addressed to Daniel — or rather, to "The Hollis Estate." Maya picks it up. She does not open it. She puts it back down.

## Beat content (planned)

The envelope on the counter. The Frank Mendel return-address. The pause. Maya's hand on the envelope. The thought, *not tonight.* The thought, *I will look at this with Sarah in the morning.* The letter back on the counter, exactly where she found it.

Sarah's headlights in the window outside.
