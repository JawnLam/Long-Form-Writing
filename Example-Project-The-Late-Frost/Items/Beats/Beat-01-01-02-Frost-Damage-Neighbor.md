# Beat 01-01-02 — Frost Damage on a Neighbor's Vines

## Purpose

**Plant Promise 1 (the frost-warning forecast).** Maya, slowing for a curve, sees blackened bud-tips on a neighbor's vine row — the unmistakable visual evidence of a recent frost event. She notes it. She is not a vintner herself but she grew up on a vineyard. She knows what she's looking at.

## Beat content (brief)

The neighbor's vine row at the edge of Maya's vision. The browned shoots. The momentary calculation: when was the last frost, how cold did it get, is it still bud-break here. Then the next curve and the moment passes. But the moment was real and the reader has registered it.

## Connections

- **Plants:** Promise 1 in [[_promises]]
- **Motif touch:** [[The-Late-Frost]] — first appearance; emerging status
- **Foreshadows:** Chapter 3 (the first hard cold night) and the harvest-fate question

## Notes

- This is a load-bearing planting beat. The frost has to feel observed (Maya's vintner-eye knowledge), not announced. The reader who doesn't know about bud-break biology should still register the dread on Maya's behalf via the lean toward the shoulder, the inhale, the not-quite-comment
- Resist the urge to explain the biology here. Save it for Chapter 3 when Hector and Sarah are working the wind machines
