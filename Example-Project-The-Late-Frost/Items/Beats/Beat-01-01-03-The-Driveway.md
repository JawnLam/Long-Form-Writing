# Beat 01-01-03 — The Driveway

## Purpose

Maya turns into the vineyard's long driveway. Establish the property's specific geography (the curve of the drive, the heritage Zin block on the left, the main house at the rise, the manager's cottage further back). Plant Hector's offstage presence (his truck visible at the cottage). Slow the approach so the empty-house feeling is set up before the empty house appears.

## Beat content (brief)

The dust on the windshield from the gravel drive. The first sight of the Zin block — the heritage block, the one Maya's grandfather planted in 1972. Hector's truck visible at the cottage. The main house at the rise. Maya parks. She does not get out immediately.

## Notes

- Hector's truck is the only signal of life on the property in this beat. It does its work and then Maya is alone again
- The Zin block's first naming. This is a quiet plant — Zin block as the *heritage* stake (per [[_promises]] anticipated promise). Not load-bearing yet
- The pause before getting out is a small but earned beat — the writer is honest that arriving is its own emotional weight
