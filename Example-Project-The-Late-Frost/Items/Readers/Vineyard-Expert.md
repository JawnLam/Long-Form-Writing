# Reader — Vineyard Expert

## Who they are

A working winemaker or vineyard manager in Sonoma or Napa. Has thirty years in the industry. Reads novels set in their world with a combination of curiosity and prickly suspicion. Catches every error.

## What they want from this novel

- Technical accuracy in the small details — the right grape varietals for the AVA, the right frost-defense equipment for the era and price-point, the right vocabulary for the work
- A respectful portrait of vineyard labor — they want the work of the season to be *seen*, not romanticized into "wine country" lifestyle prose
- Hector Reyes (vineyard manager) to be a person, not a prop — they have read too many novels set in wine country where the manager is a service-class fixture rather than a working professional

## What they fear in this novel

- "Wine country" prose — descriptions that sound like marketing copy for a tasting room
- Frost-defense technical errors — wind machines used at temperatures where they're useless, smudge pots described as primary defense rather than supplemental, fundamental misunderstanding of bud-break biology
- The vineyard manager portrayed as folksy or noble-laborer

## Specific watch items

- **Frost biology:** primary buds, secondary buds, the 28°F threshold, the 3-hour exposure window, the 10-day assessment lag. Per [[_continuity]] world-rules. The vineyard expert will catch any deviation.
- **Equipment:** wind machines (mainstream, used at the right temperatures), propane heating systems (newer, expensive, under loan in this novel's case — accurate to the era), smudge pots (heritage equipment, oil-burning, marginally effective, used here in the Zin block for both literal frost defense and emotional inheritance)
- **Cab-Zin-Sauv mix:** plausible for the Sonoma AVA at this scale; the Zin block as heritage planting is accurate to the family-vineyard pattern in the region

## Simulation prompts for the AI

When asked to do READER-SIMULATION with this reader:

- Surface any technical claim that is unsourced — every frost-defense or biology detail should trace back to either [[_continuity]] world-rules or an explicit WORLDBUILDING-session source
- Surface any sentence that sounds like tasting-room copy
- Surface any moment where Hector is doing the work of his role and the prose is treating him as scenery rather than as a person

## Notes

- This reader is secondary, not primary — the literary-fiction reader's experience is the higher priority — but technical errors will surface to the literary-fiction reader as wrongness even if they can't name what
- The WORLDBUILDING session before Chapter 3 ("The First Cold Night") should specifically use this reader as the audit lens
