## Sub-periods

### Childhood at the vineyard (1986–1998)

The years before her mother died. The family was four. Maya's earliest memories are vineyard-shaped.

### The shielding years (1998–2002)

After Helen's death. Maya at 14 steps into the older-sister-as-quasi-parent role. The four years of high school during which the structural distortion of the sister-relationship sets.

### Departure (2002–2009)

College, graduate school, Portland. The deliberate distance.

### Adult life (2009–2025)

Career, marriage, motherhood. The vineyard as occasional weekend visit.

### Inheritance (2025–2026)

The nine months between Daniel's death and the novel's opening. Estate-management from Portland. The Mendel letter sits unopened at the vineyard.
