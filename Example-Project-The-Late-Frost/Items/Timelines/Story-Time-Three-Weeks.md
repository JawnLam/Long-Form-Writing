# Story-Time — Three Weeks of Frost-Watch

## What this timeline tracks

The novel's story-time: the twenty-two days from Maya's arrival at the vineyard to the morning the harvest's fate is settled. Source-of-truth for the per-day chronology of plot events. Mirrors the timeline section in `_continuity.md` but in Item-form for cross-layer reference (this Timeline Item is referenced by character-specific Timelines for both sisters and by `_continuity.md`'s reconciliation table).

## Sub-periods

### Week 1 — Day 1 to Day 7

The frost arrives. The sisters establish the working pattern. The letter sits on the counter unopened.

### Week 2 — Day 8 to Day 14

The discovery. The confrontation. The continued work under the new condition. The buyer's call at the end of the week is the inflection.

### Week 3 — Day 15 to Day 22

The deepening. The fight about the father (Chapter 10). The frost passes. The decision lands. The morning after.
