# Family History — The Hollis Vineyard (1968–2026)

## What this timeline tracks

The fifty-eight years of Hollis family vineyard ownership. Source-of-truth for the pre-novel backstory that informs many Scenes (the heritage Zin block; the loans; the parents' deaths; Hector's tenure). Cross-referenced by the character-specific Timelines for Maya and Sarah.

This is `lfw_timeline_layer: world-history` even though the "world" is contemporary California — the *world* of this manuscript includes the vineyard's history, which is its world for storytelling purposes.

## Sub-periods

### Founding (1968–1992) — William Hollis era

The grandfather's vineyard. The heritage Zin block dates from this period. The family's identity as a wine-country family is forged here.

### Daniel and Helen (1986–1998) — the family era

The years the children were born and the marriage was alive. Twelve years. Helen's death in 1998 closes this era abruptly.

### Daniel alone (1998–2025) — the long widowhood

Twenty-seven years. Daniel runs the vineyard alone. Withholding becomes the family's structural posture; he raises both daughters in it.

### The debt era (2019–2025) — the silent borrowing

The six years Daniel borrowed without telling either daughter. The vineyard's slow approach to its current financial position. This is the era the novel will dramatize through retrospect.

### The interregnum (July 2025 – April 2026) — Daniel's death to Maya's arrival

The nine months between Daniel's death and the novel's opening. Hector keeps operations going; Frank Mendel sends the January letter; Sarah carries the secret.
