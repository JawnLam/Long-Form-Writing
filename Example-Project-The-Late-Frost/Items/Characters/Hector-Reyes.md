# Hector Reyes

## Function in this manuscript

Vineyard manager since 2009. Lives on-site in the manager's cottage. Hector is a working professional and the operational ground beneath the sisters' relationship-plot. He is also a quiet co-witness to the family's history — present for the last fifteen years of Daniel's life, present for Sarah's two-month visit the summer before Daniel died, present for the months after Daniel's death during which the vineyard ran without an owner.

## Demographics

- **Age:** 54
- **Location:** Glen Ellen; manager's cottage on the Hollis estate
- **Profession:** Vineyard manager (degree from UC Davis, viticulture); twenty-five years in Sonoma; the last fifteen here
- **Family:** Married to Lupe; two adult children (one in Sacramento, one in Portland); a granddaughter on the way

## Function-specific notes

- **The Vineyard-Expert reader's audit lens.** Hector's portrayal must satisfy [[Vineyard-Expert]] — he is a working professional, not folksy scenery. Calibration check at every drafted Hector scene.
- **The unpaid-wages information state.** Per [[_continuity]] information-state ledger: Hector has not been paid his full salary for the last five months. Daniel was rotating partial payments. Hector has not raised it with the sisters. The reveal-timing of this fact is currently deferred to Chapter 4 (where Sarah may learn it from Hector) and Chapter 10 (where Maya may learn it).
- **The Sarah-visit information.** Hector knows Sarah was at the vineyard for two months the summer before Daniel died. He has not told Maya this. This is a continuity-tracked fact that surfaces in Sarah's POV chapters and is load-bearing in the eventual confrontation about the relationship to the father.

## Voice and behavior

- Speaks Spanish and English; uses Spanish with Lupe and his crew, English with the sisters (Daniel spoke fluent Spanish; Maya speaks restaurant-level; Sarah is more fluent than Maya but pretends not to be when Hector is present)
- Short sentences. Often replies with a single word or a single nod.
- Comfort with long silence — he will let a question sit for half a minute before answering
- Does not perform deference. Does not perform familiarity either. The relationship to the sisters is professional with twenty years of texture underneath.
