- **POV-voice-register's avoid-moves discipline.** When the writer drafted Maya's voice register first, then Sarah's, the avoid_moves for each were naturally the *other* POV's signature_moves. - **Sarah's bible deferral.** Sarah's bible would have been a substantial session by itself. I proposed deferring it to a later session and keeping the four-reason steelman in [[Sarah-Hollis]] as the load-bearing antagonist-defense. Writer agreed. Open thread logged
- **Theme atom's "What it must NOT do" specificity.** When the writer drafted [[Honesty-Under-Cost]]'s treatment-risks section, three of the four risks were *specific to this manuscript* (Maya-as-moral-center; Daniel-as-projection-screen; Chapter 5 sentimentality) rather than generic theme-treatment risks. This is the discipline the section is supposed to do; logged as the worked-example

### What the writer flagged

- **The Story Circle's beat 8 divergence.** The writer wanted to be sure the overlay-divergence was honest — *the novel actually does refuse the clean change* — rather than convenient. The divergence-notes section in the overlay file documents this so the writer holds themselves to it during drafting

Decision: literary tuning is correct; if a specific promise feels under-tracked, the writer will trigger SETUP-PAYOFF-AUDIT explicitly. The cadence default is right for this novel
