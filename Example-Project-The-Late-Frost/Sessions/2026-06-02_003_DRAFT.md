## SCENE-AUDIT flag — value-shift asserted not dramatized

After Beat 4 I ran a brief SCENE-AUDIT check on the value-shift declared in [[01-01-The-Approach]]'s frontmatter (`ignorant → suspecting`). The shift is *asserted* in the prose ("she knew something was off") but not *dramatized.* The reader is told Maya's suspicion arrived; not shown the sensory or perceptual moment of its arrival.

I proposed a revision direction: let the moment of suspicion land on the chair at the head of the dining table (a specific concrete impossibility — Daniel always pushed it in; the chair is pulled out an inch and at an angle, as if someone has been sitting in it recently). This would give [[The-Empty-Chair]] its first weight-bearing appearance one chapter earlier than the outline currently plans, which the writer accepted.

The flag is logged on [[Beat-01-01-04-Empty-House-Walkthrough]] (under SCENE-AUDIT flag — Session 003) and the revision is scheduled as the first open thread in [[_state]] for the next session.
