# Session 006 — WEATHER-CHECK on Beat 5 dread

## Activity

WEATHER-CHECK (v1.4.0; chapter 16 §2). The writer signaled dread about the upcoming Beat 5 drafting session (the letter on the kitchen counter — the planting moment for Promise 2). AI ran the WEATHER-CHECK protocol.

This is the worked-example demonstration of WEATHER-CHECK on this cartridge. The chapter 16 worked example was hypothetical; this session is what the activity looks like in practice on a real-state cartridge.

## What we did

### Step 1 — Name the weather

**AI:** What's the weather like? Name it as specifically as you can — dread, grief, doubt, boredom, burnout, overwhelm, envy, something else.

**Writer:** Dread. Specifically dread of writing Beat 5.

### Step 2 — Distinguish from adjacent technical states

**AI:** Three things this could be:

1. *Technical blockage* — you have a craft problem with the scene; the right activity is STUCK-DIAGNOSTIC
2. *Craft-as-procrastination (F18)* — the dread is masking avoidance of the prose-work; the right activity is naming the pattern and resuming
3. *Genuine affective weather* — the dread is real and specific; WEATHER-CHECK is the right activity

Which is it?

**Writer:** Affective weather. I know how to write the scene. The beat is fully planned in [[Beat-01-01-05-The-Letter]]. The dread isn't about not knowing what to do.

**AI:** Then more specifically: dread of *content* (writing about the letter / the debts is emotionally hard), dread of *getting it wrong* (the scene won't land), or dread of *committing* (once written, the novel commits to this being the inciting object)?

**Writer:** Commitment. The letter is the planting moment for Promise 2. Once I write it the whole novel commits to that being the inciting object. And it's a small physical thing on a kitchen counter. It has to do all that work.

### Step 3 — Triage scope

**AI:** Is this a today-state (will probably pass tomorrow), a this-week-state (may affect the week's cadence), a this-month-state (warrants re-pacing), or a this-project-state (warrants a larger conversation)?

**Writer:** Today. Tomorrow at most. I'll probably be fine once I write it.

### Step 4 — Smallest possible next move

**AI:** Three options:

- Write Beat 5 today, hold it loosely (status `drafting`, not `drafted`). The SCENE-AUDIT pass will do its work later. The OV's revision discipline means today's draft is not the commitment
- Defer Beat 5 today; do REVISE on Beat 4 instead (the SCENE-AUDIT flag is still open). The dread of Beat 5 commitment isn't fired today
- Walk away today; come back tomorrow. The dread of commitment is sometimes solved by a night's sleep

What feels right?

**Writer:** First option. I want to write the beat. I just needed someone to acknowledge that today's draft isn't the final commitment.

### Step 5 — Log and escalate

No escalation needed; the writer's signal is normal commitment-anxiety on a load-bearing planting moment, not severe distress.

Logged here. Revisit flag: none — the writer expects this to pass tomorrow.

## What I (AI) flagged during the session

- **The activity is short.** Twelve minutes. That's correct for WEATHER-CHECK — affective state is not solved by long sessions, and prolonged self-examination becomes its own corrosion (chapter 16 §1)
- **The writer named it as commitment-dread specifically.** The disambiguation produced specificity. "Dread" alone would have left the next move generic; "dread of commitment to a load-bearing planting moment" produces a specific next move (hold the draft loosely; the revision discipline IS the safety)
- **No affirmation produced.** The AI did not say "you've got this" or "your book is good" or "I believe in you." The activity stayed acknowledgment + diagnostic. Per chapter 16 §1 — motivation as substitute for diagnostic (F52) is the failure mode the activity guards against

## What the writer flagged

- **The acknowledgment was what they needed.** The writer noted that naming the dread as *commitment-dread* (rather than generic dread) produced relief on its own. The triage and the next-move options reinforced rather than created the relief. This matches chapter 16 §2's design — the work happens in Step 1's specificity, not in Step 4's prescription
