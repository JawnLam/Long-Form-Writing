# Maya's first night back at the vineyard

> **FPED — Full Prose Exploratory Draft.** Exploratory only; not a canonical Scene until promoted.

The smudge pots were already lit when Maya's headlights swung across the yard, and for a second she thought the rows were on fire. Then the smell reached her — diesel and scorched air, the smell of every frost her father had ever fought — and she understood Sarah had been out here for hours.

Her sister didn't turn around. "You're late," Sarah said, to the vines.

"There was traffic in Napa." There hadn't been. Maya got out anyway and stood at the edge of the light, watching Sarah walk the row with a thermometer she didn't need to read; she knew the number by the ache in her hands. Twenty years, and the first thing between them was still a temperature.

"We'll know by morning," Sarah said. She said it the way their mother used to say grace — a thing you did whether or not you believed it would change the outcome.

Maya almost asked *know what.* She didn't. She had driven three hundred miles to not ask things, and she meant to be good at it for at least one night.
