# The Late Frost — Outline (v2)

## Book-level shape

Literary novel; close-third alternating POV (Maya odd chapters, Sarah even); present tense; ~75,000 words across 14 chapters. Three-week time-frame from Maya's arrival to the morning the harvest is either lost or saved.

Three-act structure (loose):

- **Act One (Chapters 1–4):** Maya arrives at the vineyard; the frost-warning is issued; the sisters meet for the first time in three years; the unopened lawyer's letter is discovered. The dramatic question is established.
- **Act Two (Chapters 5–10):** The three-week frost-watch unfolds; the sisters work the vineyard together; the debt-secret emerges in pieces; Maya decides whether to confront Sarah or to keep the secret of-knowing-the-secret.
- **Act Three (Chapters 11–14):** The frost passes or doesn't; the debt-confrontation lands; the relationship survives or doesn't; the vineyard's fate is settled.

## Parts

*No formal parts. The fourteen chapters compose the novel directly. Act boundaries are between chapters 4 / 10, but not marked structurally.*

## Structural decisions logged

### 2026-06-02 — Decision: Three-week compressed timeframe

**Decided:** The novel takes place over three weeks (the frost-watch period). Not the year-long aftermath of the inheritance; not the decade-long sibling estrangement. The compression is structural.

**Alternatives considered:**
- Year-long timeline (rejected: too much room for evasion; the compressed timeframe forces the confrontation)
- Single weekend (rejected: too short for the labor of the vineyard to do its work on the relationship)

**Rationale:** The frost is the external clock. Three weeks is long enough for the sisters to work together and short enough for the debt-secret to become unbearable.

---

### 2026-06-02 — Decision: Alternating close-third, not omniscient

**Decided:** Maya and Sarah alternate as POV across chapters; close-third, present tense. Information-state asymmetry between the sisters is the engine of the plot.

**Alternatives considered:**
- Omniscient (rejected: would dissipate the dramatic question, which depends on the reader being inside one sister at a time)
- Single POV (Maya) with Sarah opaque (rejected: Sarah's interiority — what she knows and chooses not to say — is the novel's load-bearing tension)

**Rationale:** The reader needs to be inside Sarah for the secret-keeping to be felt as a choice rather than as a withholding device. The reader needs to be inside Maya for the discovery to land emotionally.

## Open structural questions

- [ ] Should Chapter 14 end with the buyer accepting or declining? The current spine leaves the harvest fate ambiguous but commits on the relationship; the buyer question is the open one.
- [ ] Should there be a brief epilogue, or is Chapter 14's open ending the right close? Lean: no epilogue. The form's grammar wants the open close.
